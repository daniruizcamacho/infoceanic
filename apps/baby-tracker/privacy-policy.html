1. Introduction

Welcome to our Baby Tracker App. We understand the sensitive nature of the information you may input into our application. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal and baby-related data.

2. Data We Collect

The application allows you to track metrics such as feeding, sleep, and diaper changes. All data entered into the app is stored locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or store this sensitive data on our servers.

3. Data Backup and Sync

If you choose to enable cloud backups (e.g., through Android Auto Backup or manual exports), your data will be synchronized to your personal cloud account (like Google Drive). We do not have access to these backups.

4. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services for crash reporting and analytics to improve the app's performance. These services may collect anonymous usage data. We ensure any third-party service used complies with strict privacy standards.
